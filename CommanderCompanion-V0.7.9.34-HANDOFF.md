# Commander Companion V0.7.9.34 — Handoff / Save Point

Authoritative build after the final visual-lock pass.

## Locked visual changes
- Bottom chassis is rendered at its native 976:320 proportion; center jewel is round.
- Six equal utility buttons: Home, Card ID, Game Chat, Player Rescue, Settings, Profile.
- Smart jewel pulse/flash preserved with circular 1:1 overlays.
- Next Phase / End Turn receive the same attention pulse only when smart guidance marks that progression as the sole remaining step.
- Battlefield inner tray art removed; permanents centered and proportional; vertical dead space reduced.
- Normal player-card text is white; life heart is always red; hand/library icons are white.
- Mana pips stay square. Mana numbers are green for floating excess, white when equal to represented battlefield mana, red when below represented battlefield mana. Never player-colored/blue.
- Landing page remains locked and unchanged except displayed version number.

## Preservation rule
Do not redesign adjacent components. Future work begins engine tuning from this visual baseline unless explicitly unlocked.
